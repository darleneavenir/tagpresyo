Where these numbers come from

TagPresyo's benchmarks are taken from published pricing guidance, not from live market data. Figures move, so treat them as a starting point and check the current rate for anything you are charged.

References

  • The Business Manual — Pricing Guide for Filipino Business Owners. Cost-plus, value-based and competitive pricing; markup of 30–100% for retail, 50–200% for handmade, 20–50% for retailers; the 8% flat tax above ₱250,000 annual gross.
    thebusinessmanual.ph/business-101/strategy/pricing-guide-filipino-business-owners
  • Toast — How to Calculate Food Cost Percentage. Food cost of 30–35% of the selling price as the common rule of thumb, against National Restaurant Association medians of 32.0% and 32.4%.
    pos.toasttab.com/blog/on-the-line/how-to-calculate-food-cost-percentage
  • Shopify Philippines — Product Cost. Direct materials plus direct labor plus indirect costs and overhead equals total product cost, divided by units made for the cost of one. This is the structure the Costing Guide follows.
    shopify.com/ph/blog/product-cost
  • Shopee Philippines Help Centre — Seller Fees. The 2.24% transaction fee and the ₱5 order processing fee, with the first 50 orders a month waived. Commission varies by category.
    help.shopee.ph/portal/4/article/77272
  • Upwork — How to Set Your Freelance Rate. Target income divided by working hours, then adjusted for roughly 60% billable time and annual business expenses. This is the method behind the "help me figure it out" hourly rate.
    upwork.com/resources/how-to-set-your-freelance-rate
  • Joto Imaging Supplies — How to Build a Profitable Pricing Strategy for Your Print Shop. Margins of 100–150% on mugs and coasters, 150–250% on shirts and hoodies, 200–300% on personalized gifts, 50–100% on bulk orders, and the cost categories a print shop forgets.
    jotoimagingsupplies.com/blogs/blogs/how-to-build-a-profitable-pricing-strategy-for-your-print-shop
