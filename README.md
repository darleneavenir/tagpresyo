# tagpresyo

TagPresyo

A Beginner-Friendly Costing and Pricing Assistant for Filipino Micro and Small Enterprises

A high-fidelity working prototype built for IT143 Human-Computer Interaction at Mapúa University.

What it does

Most small business owners set a price by copying a competitor or adding a round number on top of what they paid for materials. Their own labor, electricity, packaging, delivery and marketplace fees never make it into the number, because no separate receipt ever arrives for them.

TagPresyo asks one plain question at a time, does the arithmetic, and shows the working:

Costing Guide — six guided steps that build the true cost of making one item
Selling Price — suggests a price, shows the markup used, explains the result in money, and warns before you price below cost
Products and Services — your saved price list, with a health badge on every row
Quotations — draft, sent, accepted, downpayment, paid, ending in a printable receipt
Sales and Insights — revenue, cost, profit and margin by month, year or all time
Try it

Open the link above and click Use demo account on the login screen. It skips the sign-up and loads 15 demo items across Product, Service, and Product + Service.

Please open it on a laptop or desktop. The mobile layout is not implemented in this version.

How it was built

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in a single file, with no external libraries and no server. Data is held in the browser's local storage, so your work stays on your own machine.

Known limitations

This is a course prototype, and these are documented rather than hidden:

No mobile layout — navigation is unreachable below 900px wide
No keyboard shortcuts
No immediate undo message; reversal happens through the Recycle Bin
Single user, no cloud sync, no real email, printing or payment processing
